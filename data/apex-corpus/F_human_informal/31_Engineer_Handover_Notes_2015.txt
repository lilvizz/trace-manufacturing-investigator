Quick notes on Press-07 before I move over to the Line 12 project full
time. Ravi/IT already transferred the shared drive folders, but a few
things worth knowing that aren't obvious from just reading the files:

- Cure zone UCL is 42.3C, been that way since the PX-200 days (long
  before either of us). There's an old eng note from 2001 in the
  archive if you ever need the background -- basically PX-200 had some
  overshoot problems above ~42C on long runs.

- I ran a characterization study on the PX-400 back in early 2014
  (QUAL-2014-009 if you want to pull it) mainly because Manufacturing
  Engg wanted to know if we had room to play with cycle time. Short
  version: PX-400's control loop is a lot better than PX-200 ever was,
  it holds steady well past 42.3, no overshoot issues at all through
  44, maybe a touch of quality drop-off right at 45.

- That said, I never took it further -- nobody raised the ECO to
  actually change the number, and honestly with the SOP audit and the
  fixture change eating up my time that quarter it just didn't happen.
  V. Iyer saw the study and I don't remember him pushing back on the
  data, it just wasn't a priority at the time given yields were already
  good.

- My honest take: leave it as-is until someone runs it through change
  control properly. The data's worth another look, but that's not the
  same as a reason to touch the number.

- Other stuff: fixture (FX-7714-B) has been solid since 2014, no
  complaints. RTD #2 position tends to drift faster than the others,
  keep an eye on it at cal time.

Good luck, ping me if you get stuck on anything Line 12-related :)
