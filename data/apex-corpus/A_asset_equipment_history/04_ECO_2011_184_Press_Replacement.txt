Originator: V. Iyer, Sr. Manufacturing Engineer          Date Raised: 2011-04-02
Effective Date: 2011-08-15

1. TITLE
Replacement of PX-200 thermal curing press with PX-400 at Press-07 (Line 7, Asset 7714)

2. REASON FOR CHANGE
PX-200 (installed 1999) is approaching end-of-support. Vendor (Halberd Process
Systems) has confirmed spare parts for PX-200 controller and heater assemblies
will no longer be manufactured beyond 2012. Maintenance has flagged increasing
service risk (ref. WO-09-0640, WO-11-0090). Replacement with current-generation
PX-400 platform is recommended to maintain production continuity.

3. SCOPE OF CHANGE
  3.1 Physical removal of PX-200 unit from Press-07 position.
  3.2 Installation of PX-400 unit (same footprint, upgraded controller HPS-CTRL v3.2).
  3.3 Recommissioning and qualification of PX-400 for existing product families
      (PC-Series precision polymer housings).

4. PROCESS PARAMETERS
  This change replaces equipment only. Existing validated process parameters,
  including the cure-zone upper control limit of 42.3 deg C (per SOP-CURE-07 v5
  and SPC configuration in force at time of this ECO), are to be CARRIED FORWARD
  UNCHANGED for the qualification run. Any parameter changes beyond equipment-name
  updates would require a separate ECO and re-validation.

5. AFFECTED DOCUMENTS
  - SOP-CURE-07 (equipment reference update only, see rev. history)
  - SPC configuration (equipment ID update)
  - Asset Master Record (Asset 7714)

6. QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENT
  New equipment to be qualified per standard Process Qualification Protocol
  before release to full production. See PX400_Qualification_2011 (Engineering).
