Periodic reliability review per Corporate Reliability Policy RP-002,
covering equipment performance since installation (2011).

2. AVAILABILITY & MTBF
  Uptime (2021): 97.8%
  MTBF (2021): 412 hours
  Unplanned downtime events (2021): 4 (total 38 hrs) -- root causes:
    2x fixture-related minor jams (see WO log)

3. OPERATING PARAMETER TRENDS
  Cure-zone temperature has operated within 36.0-42.3 deg C control
  band consistently since 2011 install, with no sustained excursions.
  This is noted as a contributing factor to the equipment's strong
  reliability performance (operating comfortably within its control
  band, well below rated maximum of 70 deg C per vendor spec).

4. RECOMMENDATIONS
  - Continue current preventive maintenance schedule (90-day inspection
    cycle).
  - No reliability-driven changes to process parameters are indicated
    at this time.
  - Monitor RTD sensor #2 position (historically shows faster drift,
    see calibration history) for early signs of recurring drift.
