Title: Calibration of Process Temperature Sensors (RTD/Thermocouple)
Effective Date: 01-MAY-2020
Owner: Metrology Department                   Page 1 of 3

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines the standard method for calibration of RTD and
thermocouple sensors used in process temperature monitoring across all
Plant 2 production lines.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all process temperature sensors plant-wide, including but
not limited to Line 3, Line 5, and Line 7 thermal equipment.

3. CALIBRATION INTERVAL
Standard interval: 12 months, unless equipment-specific procedure
specifies otherwise. Sensors in high-duty-cycle service (>16 hr/day
average) may be placed on 6-month interval at Process Engineering's
discretion.

4. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
  As-found deviation: <= 0.30 deg C from reference standard (PASS)
  As-found deviation: > 0.30 deg C (FAIL -- sensor to be adjusted or
    replaced)
  As-left deviation (post-adjustment): <= 0.05 deg C

5. PROCEDURE
  5.1 Remove sensor or use in-situ comparison method per equipment type.
  5.2 Compare against certified reference standard (traceable to
      national standards, cert renewed annually).
  5.3 Record as-found reading.
  5.4 Adjust/replace if outside tolerance.
  5.5 Record as-left reading.
  5.6 Update calibration record and set next-due date.

6. RECORDS
Calibration records to be retained for minimum 7 years per Corporate
Quality Policy QP-009 (Record Retention).
