APEX THERMAL COMPONENTS -- CHENNAI PLANT 2
MAINTENANCE WORK ORDER COMPLETION REPORT

WO No: WO-18-0322                                     Date Completed: 12-SEP-2018
Equipment: Press-07 / PX-400 / Asset 7714
Technician: D. Moorthy
Supervisor Sign-off: R. Palanisamy

ISSUE
During annual calibration (see Temperature_Sensor_Calibration_History,
2017 cycle), RTD sensor "RTD-7714-2" (zone 2) showed a marginal as-found
deviation of 0.24 deg C and was flagged for monitoring. At the 2018
calibration cycle, the same sensor showed as-found deviation of 0.31
deg C, exceeding the 0.30 deg C acceptance threshold per
Calibration_Procedure_v4. Sensor failed calibration.

ACTION TAKEN
Replaced failed RTD sensor with new unit (designated "S-7714-2" /
RTD-7714-2(new) in subsequent calibration records). New sensor
calibrated immediately post-installation: as-found N/A (new), as-left
deviation 0.01 deg C. PASS.

VERIFICATION
Ran 3 production batches post-replacement (batches 88214-88216).
All within normal quality parameters. No process parameter changes
made or required as part of this replacement -- like-for-like sensor
swap.

NOTES
This is a routine sensor-drift replacement, consistent with expected
RTD service life (~6 years for zone 2 position, which runs slightly
hotter than zones 1/3 due to proximity to heater element). Not
indicative of any process control issue.
