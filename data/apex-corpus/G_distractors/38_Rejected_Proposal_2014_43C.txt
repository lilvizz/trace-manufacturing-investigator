APEX THERMAL COMPONENTS -- CHENNAI PLANT 2
PROCESS CHANGE PROPOSAL

Proposal No: PCP-2014-022                             Date Submitted: 02-MAY-2014
Proposed by: Dr. R. Padmanabhan, Process Engineer
Title: Proposed increase of Press-07 cure-zone UCL from 42.3 C to 43.0 C

1. PROPOSAL SUMMARY
Following characterization data (QUAL-2014-009) showing stable PX-400
control through at least 44 C, propose raising the production UCL
modestly to 43.0 C to allow a small cure-time reduction, supporting
Q3 2014 throughput targets on Line 7.

2. ESTIMATED BENEFIT
Cure time reduction of approx. 4 sec/cycle -> ~4.5% throughput
improvement on Press-07.

3. REQUIRED ACTIONS IF APPROVED
  - Formal ECO raising SOP-CURE-07 and SPC configuration change
  - Updated first-article inspection run (minimum 3 lots)
  - Customer notification review (per contract terms for 2 of 5
    customers on this product line, which reference "process
    parameters as qualified at time of PPAP" in supply agreements)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
DISPOSITION: REJECTED (for this cycle)
Reviewed by: V. Iyer, Sr. Manufacturing Engineer               Date: 22-MAY-2014

Reason for rejection:
The proposed change would trigger a customer notification/re-approval
requirement under existing supply agreements for two major accounts
(see Section 3), with an estimated 6-8 week customer PPAP re-approval
cycle. Given Q3 targets are only 6 weeks out, the timeline does not
support pursuing this change for the current throughput initiative.
Q3 target will instead be met via the fixture/loading sequence
efficiency gains already implemented (ECO-2014-117) and minor line
balancing.

This is a scheduling/commercial decision, not a technical rejection of
the underlying characterization data. Proposal may be revisited for a
future planning cycle with adequate lead time for customer
re-approval, should throughput pressure persist.
