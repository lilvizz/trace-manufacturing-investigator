APEX THERMAL COMPONENTS -- DESIGN ENGINEERING
PRODUCT THERMAL SPECIFICATION

Doc No: SPEC-PRODB-TH-01                              Date: 01-SEP-2013
Product: Product B (precision polymer connector housing, non-PC-Series)
Prepared by: Design Engineering Dept

1. THERMAL PROCESSING REQUIREMENT
Product B's polymer compound (Grade PB-7, different resin formulation
from the PC-Series material used on Press-07/Line 7) has a recommended
maximum processing temperature of 42.0 deg C during thermal cure, per
resin manufacturer datasheet (ResinCo Technical Bulletin TB-114, 2012),
to avoid discoloration in this specific colorway.

2. APPLICABLE EQUIPMENT
Product B is produced on Line 4 (Press-04, Equipment PX-350), not on
Line 7. This specification applies only to Product B production and
has no bearing on other products, equipment, or lines.

3. NOTE
This value (42.0 C) is a material-property-driven limit specific to the
PB-7 resin's discoloration threshold, established via the resin
manufacturer's datasheet rather than an internal thermal characterization
study.
