Title: Plant-Wide Temperature Control Requirements for Thermal Processes
Owner: Corporate Quality Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes minimum requirements for the management of
temperature control limits across all thermal processing equipment at
Apex Thermal Components facilities.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all thermal curing, molding, and forming processes across
all plants.

3. REQUIREMENTS
  3.1 Each thermal process shall have documented upper and lower control
      limits, established through process validation appropriate to the
      specific equipment, material, and product.
  3.2 Control limits shall be reviewed at intervals not exceeding 5
      years, or upon any equipment change, material change, or process
      change, whichever is sooner.
  3.3 Where a review identifies that the technical basis for an existing
      limit may no longer be current (e.g., following an equipment
      change), the responsible Process Engineering function shall
      determine whether re-validation is warranted and document the
      determination, per local site change control procedures.
  3.4 This policy does not itself establish or change any specific
      numeric limit for any specific process; numeric limits remain
      the responsibility of local Process Engineering and Quality
      functions per site-level procedures (e.g., QP-002, QP-004 at
      Chennai Plant 2).

4. RATIONALE
Consistent, plant-wide expectations for temperature control governance
support audit readiness and reduce risk of undocumented or
unsubstantiated process limits across the organization.

5. APPROVAL
Corporate Quality Office -- 01-Jan-2016
