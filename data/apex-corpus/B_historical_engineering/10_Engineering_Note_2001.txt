To: P. Sundaram (Quality), K. Doraiswamy (Plant Engg), M. Elango (Production)
Subject: Press 7 (PX-200) cure zone -- revised upper operating boundary

Further to the characterization study (PE-STUDY-2001-07), investigation
report (INV-2001-014), and Quality review of 14-Jun-2001 (ref QM-2001-06),
this note confirms the revised upper operating boundary for the Press 7
cure zone.

Study data showed onset of thermal instability (controller overshoot,
part quality effects) developing above approximately 42 deg C during
extended (multi-hour) production runs, with the effect becoming clearly
problematic by 43 deg C.

To provide adequate margin below this onset while minimizing unnecessary
impact to cycle time, the following boundary is established:

    Cure zone upper control limit (UCL):  42.3 deg C

This reflects the upper edge of the region confirmed stable across all
extended trial runs in PE-STUDY-2001-07 (the 42 deg C trial showed only
minor, self-correcting drift with no quality impact, whereas 43 deg C
showed clear degradation) plus a small additional margin (+0.3 C) to
account for normal sensor/controller tolerance, consistent with our
standard practice of not setting operating limits exactly at a trial
setpoint value.

Please update SOP-CURE-07 and the SPC configuration accordingly. The
interim 4-hr continuous-run cap (per M. Elango, 25-Apr-01) may be lifted
once the new limit is in effect and confirmed via SPC.

- N. Ramachandran
