Effective Date: 2014-03-03

1. TITLE
Replacement of part-locating fixture on Press-07 (Line 7) -- tooling upgrade

2. REASON FOR CHANGE
Existing part-locating fixture (Tooling ID FX-7714-A, in service since 2011
install) shows wear at locating pins causing occasional minor part
misalignment (see Quality nonconformance log, Q4 2013, 6 occurrences).
Replacement with upgraded fixture design (FX-7714-B, hardened steel
locating pins) recommended to reduce misalignment defects.

3. SCOPE OF CHANGE
  3.1 Removal of fixture FX-7714-A.
  3.2 Installation of fixture FX-7714-B (drop-in replacement, same mounting
      interface).
  3.3 First-article inspection of 20 parts post-installation.

4. PROCESS PARAMETERS
  No change to thermal process parameters. Cure-zone UCL remains 42.3 deg C
  per SOP-CURE-07 v5 (unaffected by this change).

5. AFFECTED DOCUMENTS
  - Tooling register (FX-7714-A retired, FX-7714-B added)
  - No SOP or SPC changes required.
