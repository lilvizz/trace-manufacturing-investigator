AGENDA ITEM 1: Press 7 high-temp alarm events (Apr & Jun)

Discussion:
- S. Ganesan summarized WO-01-0142 and WO-01-0198 -- no mechanical fault
  found, both occurred during long continuous runs.
- Dr. Ramachandran presented preliminary findings from ongoing thermal
  characterization study (informal, full study report to follow) --
  process shows instability developing above roughly 42 C during
  multi-hour runs, appears linked to zone-2 heater response lag.
- General agreement that the interim 45 C limit (set at 1999 install,
  see Installation Record) does not provide enough margin, esp. for full
  shift-length runs.
- K. Doraiswamy: agrees a more conservative production limit is needed.
  Asked whether we can just drop straight to 40 C to be safe.
- Dr. Ramachandran: felt 40 C may be overly conservative and could affect
  cycle time / throughput unnecessarily; preferred to set the boundary
  with a defined margin below the onset of instability rather than an
  arbitrary round number, once study data is finalized.
- M. Elango: raised that Production needs a firm number soon, currently
  running to a verbal 4-hr continuous-run cap per interim instruction,
  which is affecting scheduling.

DECISION:
- Agreed IN PRINCIPLE to establish a conservative upper production
  boundary below the observed instability region, to be finalized by
  Process Engg once the characterization study and investigation report
  are complete.
- Dr. Ramachandran to issue a short engineering note confirming the final
  number and margin basis, target: early Sept 2001 (allowing time to
  finish the multi-week extended trials at the higher setpoints).
- Interim 4-hr continuous run cap (per M. Elango's verbal instruction,
  25-Apr-01) to remain in force until the formal note is issued.
