APEX THERMAL COMPONENTS -- CHENNAI PLANT 2
PROCESS VALIDATION UPDATE

Report No: PE-VAL-2004-03                              Date: 19-MAY-2004
Prepared by: S. Venkataraman, Process Engineer
Reviewed by: P. Sundaram, Quality Manager

1. PURPOSE
Periodic (3-yearly) confirmation of process validation status for Press 7
(PX-200) cure process, per Corporate Quality Policy QP-004 (revalidation
interval requirement).

2. SCOPE
Confirm that the cure process continues to perform within the established
operating boundary (UCL 42.3 deg C, per Engineering Note ENG-NOTE-2001-09)
under current production conditions.

3. METHOD
Reviewed 24 months of SPC data (2002-2004) for cure-zone temperature and
associated part quality metrics (blister rate, dimensional conformance).
No new extended-range trials were conducted as part of this update; this
is a confirmation of continued stability under the existing boundary,
not a re-derivation of the boundary itself.

4. FINDINGS
  - Cure-zone temperature has remained within 36.0-42.3 deg C for
    >99.7% of production time over the review period.
  - Blister rate has remained below 0.5% throughout, consistent with
    stable operation.
  - No high-temperature alarm events recorded since the 2001 boundary
    revision (compare to 2 events in the 6 months prior to the revision).
  - Occasional brief excursions to 42.4-42.6 deg C noted (11 instances
    over 24 months), all self-corrected within 2 minutes, none associated
    with quality impact. Not considered significant.

5. CONCLUSION
The existing operating boundary (UCL 42.3 deg C, established 2001)
continues to be appropriate for current production conditions on the
PX-200 unit. No change recommended.

6. NEXT REVIEW
Per QP-004, next periodic revalidation due 2007 or upon any equipment/
process change, whichever comes first.

7. DISTRIBUTION
Quality file, Process Engineering file
